Dewey on iPhone Duo

The open phone's 7.6" inner display. It reports a regular width, so the tab bar turns into a sidebar and each tab shows its list and the thing you opened side by side. Folded, the 5.4" outer display keeps the phone layout, with three tweaks for its shorter screen (Folded, at the bottom). Same Dew theme, type and picks (G1, A1, T1); nothing new to learn, just no more going back and forth.

  • The hinge (dashed line) runs down the middle. Sidebar + list fill the left half and the detail fills the right, so nothing important sits on the crease.
  • The sidebar holds the five tabs with their counts, then your classes. Tapping a class filters the list. It slides away (top-left button or a swipe) to give the list and detail more room.
  • Drawn at the real shape: 2670 × 1878 px (Apple's spec) is about 890 × 626 pt, 10:7, wider than tall. The point size assumes 3× like other iPhones; Apple hasn't published it. Each half is about as wide as a phone (445 pt vs 402), so the right half is a phone-sized page and the left half fits the sidebar and a narrow list.
  • The layout is chosen by size class, never by "is it open", so a 50/50 split with another app gets the folded layout.

Folded

The 5.4" outer display: 1398 × 2034 px at 460 ppi, about 466 × 678 pt at 3× (MacRumors' figures; Apple hasn't published points). That's a little wider than an iPhone 17 (402 pt) and about 200 pt shorter, so it keeps the normal phone layout (compact width, tab bar) but fits fewer rows. Three tweaks, keyed to height rather than "is it folded", so they also help small iPhones and landscape split view:

  • To Do: the count buttons and week strip scroll away with the list into a one-line bar; scroll up or tap the bar to get them back.
  • Assignment: a shorter date-due card (one ruled row, smaller stamp) so Instructions start above the bottom bar.
  • Grades: no change; the wider screen gives the letter row more room.
