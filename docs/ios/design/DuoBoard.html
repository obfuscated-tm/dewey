What to judge on the Duo

  1. To Do, By class: with three columns across, is a card easy to read and are the circle and the Turn in button easy to hit?
  2. The fold runs through the middle column of the board. Does that bother you on the real screen?
  3. Tap a card: is the right half wide enough to read, and is one column beside it still useful?
  4. The rail has five tabs and nothing else. Are they big enough?
  5. The top row holds the title, By class / Calendar / Plan and the week on one line. Too tight, or fine?
  6. To Do, Calendar: the month sits left of the fold and the picked day's work right of it.
  7. Grades, Inbox and Classes: two lists side by side with the hinge between them, nothing opening by itself.
  8. Turn the Duo upright: two columns and a tab bar; a tapped item takes the whole screen.
