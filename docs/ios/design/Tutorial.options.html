How Dewey tracks your work

You just marked something Done. Here's what each step means.

To do

Done
You finished it. Nothing is handed in yet. “Done · not submitted yet” means it still needs Submit on Schoology.
Turned in
Your teacher has it. Two kinds:
Submitted on Schoology: Schoology's dropbox has your file. Dewey says this only when Schoology does.
Turned in · not on Schoology: you tapped Turn in because it went in on paper, a lab site or in class.
“Not submitted” isn't “missing”Schoology only sees its own dropbox. Paper work, lab sites and Google Assignments look “not submitted” there even when they're in. Tap Turn in and Dewey stops calling it late.
Tests: Studied
Tests aren't turned in. Tick Studied when you're ready; the night-before reminder stops.
  • Everything in one glance; easy to come back to.
  • Dense: it's a lot of reading right after a tap.

neo-plan

Optional. Dewey works fully without it.

What it is

A planner website (neo-plan.vercel.app) that lays your week out as a board, one column per class. Dewey can fill it from Schoology for you.

Who it's for

People who already plan in neo-plan, or want one board shared between this phone and a computer with the Dewey extension. If you don't have an account, skip this.

What Dewey syncs

+New work from your To Do goes onto the board.
✓Done, Studied and Turned in, both ways. The newer change wins.
✓Submitted on Schoology clears it from the board.
✓Type: HW, Test, Task or Classwork.
–Never your Schoology password, grades or messages.

Connect

  1. Sign in at neo-plan.vercel.app.
  2. Open Settings and make an extension token.
  3. Copy it (starts with np_) and paste it here.

Extension, after iOS

Same words, same choices. One catch: in the extension, Done and Turn in are stored in neo-plan, so without a token the To Do rows show no ticks at all. So:

  • Welcome card drops step 3 (Connect neo-plan) and the “neo-plan” in step 1's Settings line: two steps.
  • Settings → neo-plan page gets the same explainer above the token frame.
  • Assignment page: the quiet “Token needed” goes away when there's no token.
  • Status guide: a new Settings page “How statuses work” with the extension's words (Done, not submitted · Turned in · Submitted · Missing · Studied), and the same tip (option C) or card (A/B) on the first Done click. That only happens once neo-plan is connected.
